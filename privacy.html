Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Hi.Life ("we", "us") collects when you use hi.life, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Calculators and quizzes

Our tools run entirely in your browser. Numbers you type are not sent to us. To remember your inputs, scores, habits and net-worth snapshots between visits, we store them in your browser's local storage. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

2. Information you send us

When you submit a form (Life Upgrade Plan, results email, newsletter, contest, careers, pledge, advertising or contact), we receive the details you enter, the page you were on and basic referral information (for example the campaign link that brought you). Forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a third-party form processor. We use this information only to respond, deliver what you requested and improve the service.

3. Introductions to professionals

If — and only if — you ask for an introduction on the Life Upgrade Plan form and tick the consent box, we share your answers and contact details with one relevant professional so they can contact you. Some professionals pay us a referral fee. We never sell your information to data brokers or lead lists. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

4. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message, provided through Google's certified consent tools, before personalised ads are used.

5. Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode and only load when you press play. Once loaded, YouTube may set cookies under its own privacy policy. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts.

6. Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, use the contact form. We respond within the time required by law.

8. Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, then delete them.

9. Children

Hi.Life is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
