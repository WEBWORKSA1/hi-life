About us

Hi, life. Let's make it better.

Hi.Life exists for one reason: to make it easier for anyone to improve the six areas that make a good life, using honest information and free tools.

What we believe

  • Small upgrades compound. A better budget, an extra hour of sleep, one honest conversation — together they change a life.
  • Your numbers, your privacy. Our calculators run in your browser. We do not need your data to help you.
  • Plain English wins. No jargon, no fear tactics, no "one weird trick".
  • Balance beats extremes. Money matters, but so do health, people and purpose.

What we offer

Fourteen free tools, including the Hi.Life Score, retirement and FIRE planning, debt payoff, life insurance needs and life in weeks; short guides across money, health, career, home, relationships and mind; daily news and a weekly newsletter; and a free Life Upgrade Plan that can connect you with one vetted professional.

How we make money

Hi.Life is free to use. We earn from advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, reader support, and referral fees from some professionals we introduce when readers ask for an introduction. None of these change our tools' maths or what we write. Read our editorial policy.

Who we are

Hi.Life is run by a small independent team of writers, developers and reviewers. Want to join? See careers.

Not advice

Hi.Life provides general education, not personal financial, medical, legal or tax advice. See our disclaimer.
