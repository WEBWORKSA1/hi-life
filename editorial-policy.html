Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Our standard

Every guide must be useful, accurate and written in plain English. If a reader cannot act on it the same day, we rewrite it.

Research

We rely on primary and authoritative sources: government agencies, regulators, peer-reviewed research, recognised health bodies and established financial institutions. We avoid sensational claims and clearly note uncertainty.

Review

Money and health guides are reviewed for accuracy before publication and on a regular schedule after. We are building a panel of credentialed reviewers; you can apply to join.

Tools

Calculator formulas are standard, documented methods (for example the 4% rule, 28/36 ratios, DIME and the Mifflin–St Jeor equation). Each tool explains its assumptions on the page.

Independence

Advertisers, sponsors and partner professionals have no say over our tools' results or editorial conclusions. Sponsored content is always labelled.

Updates and corrections

Each guide shows its last update date. When we find an error, we correct it promptly. Report one via our contact form.

AI use

We may use AI tools to help draft, summarise or check content. Every published page is reviewed and edited by a human for accuracy and usefulness.
