Why measure six areas?

Life coaches have used a "wheel of life" for decades: draw your life as a wheel with several spokes and rate each one. A wheel with one long spoke and several short ones does not roll well. The Hi.Life Score uses six areas that research and everyday experience show matter most: money (security and freedom), health (sleep and movement), career (meaning and income growth), home (your environment and housing costs), relationships (connection and support) and mind (calm and direction).

How to use your result

  1. Ignore the total for a moment. Look at your lowest area — that is your biggest lever.
  2. Pick one action for that area and do it weekly for 30 days. Our guides and tools give you a starting point.
  3. Retake the quiz in a month. Small improvements in a weak area usually lift your whole score.

The Life Score is a reflection tool, not a clinical or financial assessment. If any area feels overwhelming, talking with a qualified professional is a smart next step — our free Life Upgrade Plan can help you find one.
