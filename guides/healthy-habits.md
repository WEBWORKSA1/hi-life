You do not need a total life overhaul to feel better. Small habits, repeated, change how you look, feel and age. Here are ten worth starting, one at a time.

## 1. Walk every day

Brisk walking is one of the most researched forms of exercise. Large studies link higher daily step counts with lower risk of early death, with much of the benefit coming as people move from very low activity to moderate. Start with 10–20 minutes after a meal.

## 2. Lift something twice a week

Strength training protects muscle and bone as you age, improves blood sugar control and makes daily life easier. Body-weight squats, push-ups and rows at home count.

## 3. Protect your sleep

Seven to nine hours for adults. A fixed wake time is the most powerful lever. Read our full [sleep guide](guides/sleep-better.html).

## 4. Eat protein and plants at every meal

Protein keeps you full and protects muscle; vegetables, fruit, beans and whole grains bring fibre that feeds your gut and steadies energy. A simple plate: half vegetables, a quarter protein, a quarter whole grains or starch.

## 5. Drink water first

Start the day with a glass of water and keep a bottle in sight. Swap one sugary drink a day for water or unsweetened tea.

## 6. Know your numbers

Calories are not everything, but knowing your maintenance level helps. Estimate yours with the [Calorie & BMI Calculator](calories.html). Also know your blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar — book a check-up if it has been a while.

## 7. Get outside in daylight

Morning light helps set your body clock, and time in nature is linked with lower stress. Combine it with your walk.

## 8. Limit alcohol

Less is better for sleep, weight, mood and long-term health. Try alcohol-free weekdays as a starting point.

## 9. Stand up and move every hour

Long periods of sitting are linked with poorer health, even for people who exercise. Set a reminder to stand, stretch or walk for two minutes each hour.

## 10. See people

Strong social connection is associated with longer, healthier lives. Walk with a friend and you have stacked two habits.

## How to make them stick

Pick **one** habit, attach it to something you already do, make it tiny, and track it in the [Habit Tracker](habit-tracker.html). Add the next one after two to four weeks.

*This guide is general information, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program if you have a health condition.*
