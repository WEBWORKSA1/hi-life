There is a limit to how much you can cut. There is no limit to how much you can earn. Raising your income is often the fastest way to upgrade every other area of life.

## 1. Know your market value

Research what people in your role, level and location are paid. Look at salary surveys, job ads that list pay ranges, recruiter conversations and trusted peers. Write down a realistic range. Use the [Salary Converter](salary.html) to compare hourly, contract and yearly offers fairly.

## 2. Ask for the raise — properly

- **Timing**: after a win, during review season, or when your responsibilities have grown.
- **Evidence**: a one-page list of results with numbers — money saved, revenue added, time cut, problems solved.
- **A specific number** near the top of your researched range.
- **The script**: "Over the last year I have [results]. Based on that and market data for this role, I'd like to discuss moving my salary to [number]. What would it take to get there?"
- **If the answer is no**: ask for a clear plan with goals and a date to revisit, in writing. Negotiate other things too — title, training budget, flexibility, extra leave.

## 3. Change jobs strategically

Many surveys find that people who switch employers tend to see bigger pay jumps than those who stay. You do not need to job-hop constantly, but test the market every year or two. Interviewing keeps your skills sharp and your value clear.

## 4. Build skills the market pays for

The best skills to learn are:

- **In demand**: look at job ads two levels above you. What keeps appearing?
- **Measurable**: sales, analytics, software, project management, writing that converts.
- **Leveraged by AI** rather than replaced by it: use AI tools to do more, faster.

Block three hours a week for 90 days on one skill. That is roughly 40 focused hours — enough to move from beginner to useful in many fields.

## 5. Start a side income stream

Pick something that uses a skill you already have:

- Freelancing or consulting in your field
- Tutoring or coaching
- Digital products: templates, courses, guides
- A small online business or content site
- Weekend services in your area

Set a goal and a deadline. Track hours and profit; drop what does not pay.

## 6. Protect the gains

When income rises, decide in advance where the extra goes — for example, half to savings and investing, half to life. That prevents lifestyle creep from swallowing every raise.
