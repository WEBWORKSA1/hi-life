Most resolutions are dead by February. Not because people are lazy, but because goals are vague, too many, and never translated into what to do on Tuesday. Here is a system that works.

## Start with your life in weeks

Open the [Life in Weeks calendar](life-in-weeks.html). Seeing the boxes ahead makes one thing clear: you cannot do everything, but you can do the few things that matter. Then take the [Life Score quiz](life-score.html) to spot your weakest area. That is usually the best place to aim.

## Pick one theme and up to three goals

A theme is a direction ("Year of Strength", "Year of Freedom"). Goals are specific outcomes inside it. More than three goals at once means none get enough attention.

Good goals are:

- **Specific**: "Run a 5k without stopping", not "get fit".
- **Measurable**: you can tell if you did it.
- **Time-bound**: a date.
- **Yours**: chosen because you want them, not to impress anyone.

## Turn outcomes into weekly actions

You cannot control outcomes directly; you can control inputs. For each goal, define a **weekly action**:

| Goal | Weekly action |
|---|---|
| Save an emergency fund | Automatic transfer every payday |
| Run a 5k | Three runs per week, following a plan |
| Get promoted | Two hours of skill practice + one visible win shared |
| Stronger relationships | One phone-free dinner and one call to a friend |

## Track leading indicators

Lagging indicators (weight, savings balance, promotion) move slowly. Leading indicators (workouts done, transfers made, hours practised) move every week and keep you motivated. Use the [Habit Tracker](habit-tracker.html) to tick them off.

## Use implementation intentions

Write "When X happens, I will do Y." Studies on implementation intentions show this simple format increases follow-through. Example: "When I finish dinner on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, I will put on my running shoes."

## The Sunday review (15 minutes)

1. What went well this week?
2. What got in the way?
3. What is the one thing that would make next week a success?
4. Put it in the calendar.

## Plan for failure

You will miss days. Decide now: never miss twice. If a goal no longer fits your life, change it on purpose instead of letting it fade.

## Make it social

Tell one person, join a group or work with a coach. Accountability is one of the strongest predictors of follow-through.
