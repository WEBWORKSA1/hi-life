How our tools work

Every Hi.Life tool uses a standard, documented method and shows its formula, assumptions and sources on the page. Here is the full list in one place.

Our principles

  • Standard methods first. We use well-known formulas (compound growth, the 4% rule, 28/36 affordability, DIME, Mifflin–St Jeor) rather than black boxes.
  • Ranges, not false precision. Where the future is uncertain we show a range: the compound interest tool shows ±2% returns and the retirement tool runs 1,000 market scenarios.
  • Private by design. Calculations run in your browser; nothing is sent unless you choose to email yourself results.
  • Reviewed and dated. Each tool shows when it was last checked.
