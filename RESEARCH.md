# Hi.Life — Concept, Revenue Model & 40-Site Field Research

## 1. The idea (and why this one)

**Hi.Life = the free "life-upgrade" toolkit.** One site that scores and improves the six areas of a good life — **Money, Health, Career, Home, Relationships, Mind** — with interactive tools, short guides and a free personalised **Life Upgrade Plan** that doubles as a high-value lead funnel.

| Option for "Hi.Life" | Search demand | AdSense RPM | Lead value | Repeat visits | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luxury / "high life" lifestyle magazine | Medium | Low–medium | Low | Low | Pretty, poor money |
| Generic self-help blog | High but saturated | Low | Low | Low | Commodity |
| Personal-finance only site | High | High | High | Medium | Brutal competition and the name does not say "money" |
| **Life-upgrade toolkit (money-weighted)** | High | **High** on money/insurance/mortgage pages | **High** (planners, insurance, mortgage) | **High** (quiz, trackers, points, weekly log) | **Chosen** |

## 2. How the research was done

Every site below was **opened and read page by page on 17 November 2026** (homepage or the most relevant tool/article page). Notes record what each page actually showed — navigation, tools, forms, lead funnels, trust signals, newsletter capture and UX patterns — and the right-hand column says exactly what Hi.Life built from it.

Four intended sites refused automated access and were **not** used as evidence: Investopedia, Verywell Mind, Lifehacker (all "site blocked") and Livestrong (fetch error). They were replaced with Investor.gov, the CFPB, YNAB and a second page each from Calculator.net and Omni Calculator.

## 4. Revenue model

1. **Google AdSense** (pub-6620975821265271): Auto ads + four named slots. Highest-RPM pages: Life Insurance, Home Affordability, Retirement, On Track, Money Last, Debt.
2. **Lead generation — Free Life Upgrade Plan:** 4-step scored form (A/B/C grade in subject), attribution, Life Score attached, rotating example questions, "no credit check" microcopy.
3. **Embed licensing (new):** free embeds spread the brand; branded versions for banks, credit unions, insurers and employers are scored leads.
4. **Email list (new):** 7-Day course + weekly/daily newsletter with topic choices.
5. **Pro Network, sponsorships, reader support, contests, story submissions.**

## 5. AdSense approval checklist

- 17 tools each with explainer, FAQ and a "How this tool works" block (steps, assumptions, sources, reviewed stamp); 14 guides; methodology, editorial policy, how-we-make-money, privacy, terms, disclaimer, contact, about.
- `ads.txt`; AdSense meta + script in every content page head; labelled ad slots away from form buttons.
- Sitemap, robots, breadcrumb/Article/FAQ/WebApplication schema.
- **Before applying:** connect `hi.life` (AdSense does not accept github.io sub-paths) and enable the EEA/UK consent message.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| YMYL scrutiny | Sources and assumptions on every tool, disclaimers, methodology, dated review stamps |
| AI answers reducing clicks | Tool-first: calculators, stress tests, trackers and logs can't be replaced by a snippet |
| Trademark confusion with "Hi-Life"/"HiLife" | Always "Hi.Life", no third-party marks, explicit non-affiliation disclosure |
| Lead quality | Multi-step scoring, consent, one exclusive intro |
| Email exposure | XOR-encoded inbox decoded only at send time |
