# Hi.Life — Concept, Revenue Model & Benchmark Research

## 1. The idea (and why this one)

**Hi.Life = the free "life-upgrade" toolkit.** One site that scores and improves the six areas that make a good life — **Money, Health, Career, Home, Relationships, Mind** — with interactive tools, short guides and a free personalised **Life Upgrade Plan** that doubles as a high-value lead funnel.

Why this beats the alternatives for this domain:

| Option for "Hi.Life" | Search demand | AdSense RPM | Lead value | Repeat visits | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Luxury / "high life" lifestyle magazine | Medium | Low–medium | Low | Low | Pretty, poor money |
| Generic self-help blog | High but saturated | Low | Low | Low | Commodity |
| Personal-finance only site | High | High | High | Medium | Brutal competition (NerdWallet, Bankrate) and the name does not say "money" |
| **Life-upgrade toolkit (money-weighted)** | High (calculators + "how to" across 6 areas) | **High** on money/insurance/mortgage pages, medium elsewhere | **High** (financial planners, insurance, mortgage) | **High** (quiz, trackers, net-worth snapshots, daily tip) | **Chosen** |

The brand reads naturally ("Hi, life" / "high life"), so the site can rank for broad life topics while the revenue is concentrated where advertisers pay most: insurance, mortgages, retirement, investing and debt — consistently listed among the highest-CPC AdSense verticals.

## 2. Revenue model

1. **Google AdSense** (pub-6620975821265271): Auto ads + four named slots (`in-content`, `sidebar`, `home-mid`, `tool-result`). Highest-RPM pages by design: Life Insurance Needs, Home Affordability, Retirement & FIRE, Debt Payoff, the related guides.
2. **Lead generation — Free Life Upgrade Plan** (`plan.html`): 4-step form with lead scoring (A/B/C grade in the email subject), first-touch attribution (UTM/gclid/referrer), Life Score attached. Exclusive introductions sold to:
   - financial planners, insurance brokers, mortgage brokers (highest value);
   - career, health and life coaches (volume).
3. **B2B Pro Network** (`for-pros.html`): pay-per-introduction or monthly featured listing.
4. **Sponsorships** (`advertise.html`): tool sponsorship, guide sponsorship, newsletter, contest partner; also a "website / domain acquisition" option scored highest.
5. **Reader support** (`support.html`): one-time/monthly, transparent allocation (operations, talent, prizes, promotion). Pledge form works before payment links exist.
6. **Contests** (`contests.html`): grow UGC + email list; sponsor-funded prizes.
7. **Newsletter** ("The Sunday Upgrade"): footer + results-email capture on every tool.

## 4. AdSense approval checklist (built in)

- Original, substantial content: 14 guides (~7,700 words), explainer text + FAQ on every tool, About, Editorial Policy.
- Required pages: Privacy (with Google advertising-cookie wording and opt-out links), Terms, Disclaimer, Contact, About.
- `ads.txt` at root; `<meta name="google-adsense-account">`; AdSense script in `<head>` of every page except thank-you/404.
- Clear navigation, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, breadcrumb + Article + FAQ + WebApplication schema.
- No prohibited content; ads never placed next to forms in a way that encourages accidental clicks; ad slots labelled "Advertisement".
- YMYL trust signals: disclaimers, sources of formulas, update dates, "when to see a professional".
- **Must-do before applying:** connect the custom domain `hi.life` (AdSense does not accept `github.io` sub-paths) and turn on the EEA/UK consent message in AdSense.

## 5. Risks and how they're handled

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| YMYL scrutiny (money/health) | Disclaimers, editorial policy, formula transparency, expert-review recruitment |
| AI Overviews cutting informational clicks | Tool-first site: calculators and the quiz can't be answered by a snippet |
| Trademark confusion with "Hi-Life"/"HiLife" brands | Always "Hi.Life" with the dot, no third-party logos/trade dress, explicit non-affiliation disclosure (`legal.html`) |
| Lead quality | Multi-step form, scoring, consent checkbox, one exclusive intro |
| Email exposure | Inbox stored XOR-encoded in `config.js`, decoded only at send time; never in HTML |
