Transparency

How we make money

Hi.Life is free to use and always will be. Free is only honest if you know how the lights stay on, so here it is in plain English.

1. Advertising

We show ads from Google AdSense. Ads are labelled "Advertisement" and kept away from form buttons so they are never clicked by accident. Advertisers do not choose, see or influence our content, and we do not accept ads for payday loans, gambling, unlicensed financial products or miracle health claims.

2. Referral fees from professionals

If you ask for an introduction on the Life Upgrade Plan, we introduce you to one vetted professional. Some professionals pay us a referral fee or a listing fee. You never pay more because of it, we introduce you to one professional at most, and only with your consent. We never sell lists of contacts.

3. Sponsorships

Brands can sponsor a tool, guide, newsletter or contest. Sponsored placements are always labelled "Sponsored" and sponsors never change a tool's formula or a guide's conclusions.

4. Reader support

Readers can support Hi.Life with a one-time or monthly contribution that funds operations, hiring, contest prizes and promotion.

What never changes

  • Tool maths is the same for everyone and is documented in our methodology.
  • Your tool inputs stay in your browser.
  • Editorial decisions follow our editorial policy.
