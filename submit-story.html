Community

Share your upgrade story

Hi.Life is better with real stories. Paid off a debt, changed careers, fixed your sleep, rebuilt a relationship? Tell us what you did and what happened. Selected stories are published (first name or pen name only) and enter our current contest automatically.

Guidelines

  • Your own true story, 300–900 words.
  • Specific: what you changed, how, and the result.
  • No private details about other people, no promotion or links.
  • Health and money stories are shared as experience, not advice.
  • We may edit for length and clarity and will email you before publishing.

By submitting you confirm the story is yours and agree to our terms.
