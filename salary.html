How the conversion works

Yearly pay = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. Change the weeks if you take unpaid leave or work seasonally. The after-tax column uses a single estimated rate for simplicity; real taxes depend on your country, deductions and brackets.

A raise is worth more than it looks

Because future raises are usually a percentage of your current pay, a raise today lifts every future year too. Negotiating once can be worth tens of thousands over a career. Prepare with market data, a list of results you delivered, and a specific number.

Compare job offers fairly

  • Add the value of retirement matching, bonuses and equity.
  • Subtract commuting time and costs.
  • Value health cover, paid leave and remote flexibility.

Frequently asked questions

How many working hours are in a year?

A full-time 40-hour week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours. With two weeks unpaid leave it is 2,000.

What is the difference between bi-weekly and semi-monthly?

Bi-weekly is every two weeks (26 pay periods). Semi-monthly is twice a month (24 pay periods).

Why is my take-home pay lower than this estimate?

Retirement contributions, health premiums and local taxes may also be deducted from your paycheck.
